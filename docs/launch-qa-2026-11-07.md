## Verdict

**Close, but not ready to upload today.**
- **Two hard blockers sit outside the app.** Google's reviewer can't get past sign-up, because the reviewer route isn't deployed. And the
  privacy and account-deletion pages still answer 404.
- **Eight issues in the app** should be fixed before real women install it. The worst: "Send code" needs two taps, a pregnant woman is
  shown an after-birth move done lying on her back, and a sound can start on its own. Each fix is small; together they make one
  version (39.78).
- **About 20 polish items** can wait for the next update.
- **The test suite is green.** `npm test` passed every suite. The one failure, pregyoga's rest timer (83/84) while the machine was busy
  (the same check fails that way on d3112de), passed 84/84 with the machine idle. The suites the chain had skipped after it (moves3d,
  97/97, then retire to the server and Android tests) all pass.

## B. Fix in the app before launch (Version 39.78)

Ranked by harm × how many women hit it. All were reproduced: on your phone (📱) or in the rendered sweep and its checker (✓✓).

**B1. "Send code" needs two taps 📱 (every new woman, and the reviewer).** With the keyboard up after typing her number, the first tap
on Send code does nothing. 3 out of 3 times the button jumped from y 1067 to y 1379, gained focus and sent no code. The cause is Version
39.76's `app.css:1374`, `.su:has(input:focus) .ob-av { display: none }`. The tap takes focus off the box, so the picture comes back,
the button moves 312 px down, and the click lands on empty space.
- **Fix:** keep the picture hidden for the rest of the sign-up step once she has typed (a class set on `focusin`, never removed on
  blur), or stop the button taking focus on `pointerdown`.
- Joining's name box and search results are fine (checked).

**B2. A pregnant woman is shown after-birth content in the pelvic floor trainer 📱✓✓.** At 20 weeks, opened from her plan's daily set
(from week 13, `progs/v1.js:63`), the page shows:
- the "Belly gap check" ("Lie on your back… Lift your head and shoulders");
- "See a doctor if you still leak urine 3 months after the birth";
- after-birth links.

That breaks the app's own pregnancy rule: nothing on the back after 13 weeks.
- **Cause:** `mods/post.js:961`, `born || pg ? <after-birth block>`.
- **Fix:** show it only when `born`; in pregnancy use the non-birth branch. Any new pregnancy line goes to the doctor and the
  physiotherapist.

**B3. Sakhi can reopen on Wind down and start a sound with no tap ✓✓.** When Android recreates the app (killed in the background, or
after a font-size or language change), `web.restoreState` reloads the last page. A notification route left in the old intent is
replayed (`MainActivity.java:82`, read even when `state != null`). Opening a Wind down page starts its sound, so yoga nidra or a sleep
sound can play by itself. On a shared phone, that happens out loud.
- **Fix:** read the intent's `go` only on a real new launch (`state == null`, not `FLAG_ACTIVITY_LAUNCHED_FROM_HISTORY`). Never
  auto-start a sound on a restored page.

**B4. Wind down's "now playing" bar covers the player's Pause and Next 📱✓✓.** With a sound playing, open any routine. From step 2 the
bar sits on Pause and Next, so a tap pauses or stops the sound instead. The same happens in the Kegel trainer (Pause, and Stop, which
restarts the sound), pregnancy yoga, relief routines and the monthly check-in's Save.
- **Cause:** `.cm-mini` is fixed at bottom 74 px, z 15; the controls are at 74 px, z 2.
- **Fix:** lift the controls when `body.cm-mini-on`, or dock the bar above them on player routes.

**B5. Reminders can ring up to an hour late for every new user 📱.** Android 14+ turns "Alarms and reminders" off for new installs, so
the test copy's 8 pm check-in and 6 pm move nudge were booked with a 1-hour window (`dumpsys alarm`: window +1h, no exact reason). Your
phone has the permission, which is why your walks never showed it. Sakhi asks only when she makes a daily reminder by hand
(`app.js:1051`). Otherwise there is a card deep in Me > Reminders. For folic acid (9 pm) and iron tablets this breaks the baseline
rule that a medicine rings at its own time.
- **Fix:** call `askExactNow()` once after "Start my plan" (and after Android's Allow), with the existing sheet and its words.

**B6. Screens that open scrolled down 📱.**
- (a) **Joining:** after "Continue" on the goals list, which needs scrolling on a 360 dp phone, the next step opens 247 px down.
  "Your last 3 periods" and the pill switch are hidden above the screen. The cause: `renderOnboard` returns without `scrollTo(0,0)`
  (`app.js:1220`).
- (b) **Your Sakhi after sign-up** opens 326 px down. Her name, stage, goals and "What your dates show" are above the screen. The
  cause: the sign-up gate sets `curRoute` to `your-sakhi` (`app.js:1212`), so render keeps sign-up's scroll (`app.js:1240`).
- **Fix:** scroll to the top when the joining step changes, and don't let the sign-up gate claim the route.

**B7. The Learn article "your data and privacy" contradicts the app ✓✓.** It says the diary is "on servers run by the companies that
host this app". It also mentions "Tell a person" (retired), a sharing switch, WhatsApp update messages and auto-deletion. The app's
notice says "Your entries stay only on this phone". It's live, and found by searching "delete my data". Play reviewers read privacy
claims against the Data safety form.
- **Fix:** rewrite it, in English and Hindi, from `ob_notice_1-6` and `notice_text`. It's privacy wording, not health wording.

**B8. Explore's language tile still offers Marathi, Bengali, Tamil and Telugu ✓✓** (removed in Version 39.68). Searching "marathi"
finds it, and tapping it shows only English and हिंदी.
- **Fix:** `mods/lang.js:340/365`, "Hindi or English, and hearing any screen read out".

**Also worth doing in the same version (large text, P1 for women who use big fonts) 📱✓✓.**
- At 130%, Today fits.
- At 150%, the "Sakhi" logo is cut by the Help button.
- At 200%:
  - the Help button covers the logo;
  - the week strip runs into itself ("FTODAYS");
  - the cycle number spills out of its ring;
  - the tab bar grows to 106 px and covers the players' Pause, Next and Stop and the check-in's Save, so a tap there switches tab.
- **Fix:** take the sticky offsets from the tab bar's real height (a `--tb-h` variable). Let the top bar drop Help's label at large
  text.
