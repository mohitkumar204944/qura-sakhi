# After her first day: the rest of the gaps (24 Oct 2026)

Mohit, 24 Oct, on the first-day audit (`docs/first-days-audit-2026-10-24.md`):
> "Yes looks better can you identify what are more such gaps which can be improved resulting in better user experience"

What I took from it: the same kind of audit for everything after day 1. The lenses:
- where she isn't told what will happen;
- where the screen says one thing and the app does another;
- where the plan is hidden or crowded out;
- where she's asked twice, or an answer is never used;
- where she hits a dead end.

## 0. The answer on one page

Seven themes, most important first:

1. **The companion doesn't notice what she does.**
   - Day 2 says nothing about day 1.
   - Her period arriving, the very thing her goal is about, gets no reaction.
   - The weekly look-back lives only inside My plan.
   - A woman who lapsed never sees her month-1 look-back.
   - The end of the 12 weeks isn't announced on Today: the plan just disappears.
2. **Her plan is hidden behind other cards.** For women with long cycles, the women the plan is for, "Day 39, and no
   period yet" is the top card every day. The plan's own doctor check and fresh-start card stay folded underneath for
   weeks.
3. **Numbers disagree.**
   - The check-in says "3 of 3 today" while Today says "0 of 3 done".
   - "Week 1 of 12" sits next to "8 weeks pregnant", or next to "Week 3 of 6" after a birth.
   - The end screen says "You did 12 weeks" after one active day.
   - "Usual gap 21–35 days" sits next to her own 42.
4. **The 8 pm reminder leads to the wrong place.** It opens a page made for the cycle plan: weekly minutes, strength
   days, yoga, the plate. That's wrong for the pain, heavy, PMS, pregnancy, after-birth and 40+ plans, and it isn't the
   check-in even for the cycle plan.
5. **Some plans don't fit their goal.**
   - Pregnancy shows two plans at once.
   - Trying for a baby gets the cycle plan's expectations ("your waist may start to come down").
   - The heavy-bleeding plan repeats a 21-day loop and tells a vegetarian to eat eggs, fish or meat.
   - The 40+ plan opens on a cycle countdown.
   - Results for the main goal are usually empty after 12 weeks.
6. **Two of everything.**
   - Two "check-ins".
   - "What got in the way?" is asked daily and again weekly.
   - Two ways to log the walk.
   - "Other plans" offers 21-day programmes to run on top of her 12-week plan.
7. **Privacy on a shared phone.** In discreet mode, My plan still says "More regular periods", and the plan screen talks
   about periods.

**What to do:** two small versions after V37.1 (her first day):
- **V37.2, "a companion that notices"** (themes 1–4 and 7): mostly small changes.
- **V38, "a plan that fits every goal"** (themes 5–6).

Section 3 lists them, with acceptance criteria.

**Acceptance criteria for V37.2** (extend `web/test/companion.mjs`, then run `weeks-walk.mjs` and `stages-walk.mjs`
again):
1. The 8 pm reminder opens the check-in, for every plan.
2. After a check-in with "Partly", Today and the check-in show the same count, and Today shows each answer.
3. While a plan runs, its card is first in "Sakhi says". A cycle plan never shows "no period yet" as a separate card. 40+

5. A logged period gets one line from the plan, that day.
6. After week 12, Today says the plan is done and links to results. The results say how many days she followed it, and

7. Discreet mode: no goal label, no period words, on any plan screen.
8. Hindi, a 360 px screen, and no page errors.

**New wording for the doctor** (as wording-review §15): the period line (A2), the midlife version of "no period yet"
(B1), the week-12 summary (A5), and, for V38, the trying-for-a-baby and PMS lines (E3, E6).
