## Decisions (my recommendation first)

- **D1. Where help requests go until the hand-off exists:** WhatsApp and a call button to a Qura number staffed by people (not
  the sales bot). Needs the number.
- **D2. Features awaiting approval:** hidden until approved; Qura's team still sees them with prototype labels on.
- **D3. The app's name on the phone** (notifications always show it, even in discreet mode): "Sakhi". "Qura" can tell a
  family member it's a health app.
- **D4. Fonts:** bundle the two brand fonts (one ~1 MB download, open licence). Use the phone's own fonts for Hindi and the
  other scripts.
- **D5. Where APKs are downloaded from:** a public Supabase Storage bucket (free tier: 1 GB stored, enough for testing),
  until Play.
- **D6. Qura stats:** a weekly email first; a page later if needed.
