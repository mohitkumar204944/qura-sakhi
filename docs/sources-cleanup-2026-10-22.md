   for Dr. Ayushi, in English and Hindi. Dr. Megha's number 28526 came from Qura's own database, where it is marked

   a 2011 DCGI ban on letrozole for infertility in India, and no one ever checked whether that ban still stands.

   in. Someone should call 14416 and 1-800-891-4416 once.
