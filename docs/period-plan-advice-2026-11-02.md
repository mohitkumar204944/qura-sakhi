# My period plan: one plan for period pain, heavy periods and the days before (advice, 2 Nov 2026)

Mohit, 1 Nov: "we still have one plan needs to be designed". The order agreed on 30 Oct: I had a baby (Version 39.46), then one
plan for the midlife stages (39.51), then the period goal plans (pain, heavy, PMS). Advice only, nothing built.

It starts from the researched designs in `docs/plans-design-and-build-2026-10-24-programmes.md`:
- section 1, "Easier periods" (`pain`);
- section 2, "Stronger blood" (`heavy`);
- section 3, "Calmer cycle" (`pms`).

Those three were designed as separate programmes. This advice turns them into one plan, as the midlife plan did for its goals.
It brings them up to the rules set since:
- your doctor first (39.28, 39.29), and Mohit's 1 Nov principle: "everything should lead to plan and for extended medical help
  qura doctor should be advised" (39.53);
- the minimalist baseline (CLAUDE.md, 39.27);
- one card at a time in Sakhi says (39.47);
- the chapter a week that Getting ready (39.32) and the midlife plan (39.51) use;
- Wind down (39.21 to 39.24), the relief routines in Dr. Megha's voice, and the 3D figure (39.39);
- "app size is not a barrier".

## 1. The short answer

Today the three period goals open three light plans (`web/progs/v1.js`, all version 1):

| Goal | Plan today | Daily steps |
|---|---|---|
| Painful periods | Easier periods (`pain`) | The cramps routine on period days, a walk, a bedtime |
| Heavy periods | Stronger blood (`heavy`) | Iron (the 21-day iron journey), a light walk |
| Mood and body before periods | A calmer cycle (`pms`) | Calm breath, a walk, a bedtime |

Each runs on a 12-week clock that ignores her cycle. None teaches anything, and none measures her periods one by one. A woman
with pain and heavy periods gets one of them.

The proposed plan is one new plan, **"My period plan"** (`period`, version 1), for all three goals. Its clock is **her cycle**,
not the week. Each cycle has three windows:

| Window | When | What the plan does there |
|---|---|---|
| **Before** | The 7 days before her likely start | Pain: her kit made ready, Cycle care yoga. PMS: her calm week, the day's note. Heavy: "Your period is likely in 2 days" |
| **Period** | Her logged start (or "My period started") to the day after her last bleeding or pain tap, 7 days at most | Pain: her kit, and "Pain today?" in the check-in. Heavy: "Your period today" in the check-in, a rest routine offered. PMS: the note continues |
| **Between** | The rest of her cycle | Her goal's practice, moving 3 days, strength from week 5, her goal's food |

It runs three cycles (about 12 weeks, up to 16 if a period is late), then keeps going to month 12 with one chapter a month.

Her first period goal leads: its practice runs every day and its chapters come every week. A second or third period goal joins
only in the window where it matters:
- pain joins as the kit and "Pain today?" on period days;
- heavy joins as the period note, the Hb test and an iron food;
- PMS joins as the note in the 7 days before.

| Her goal leads | Today's practice (most days) | Her goal's own steps | Her chapters |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Period pain** | Relax: Wind down's slow breaths (5 minutes), or its yoga nidra at bedtime | The kit on period days: warmth, the cramps routine, a rest position | Why periods hurt; the kit; is my pain the usual kind; moving between periods; medicines: ask, don't guess; what a doctor checks |
| **Heavy periods** | Today's iron food (the 7-day rotation by region and diet, Version 39.9) | The Hb test (weeks 1 to 2), her tablet if her doctor gave one, the period note | Is my bleeding heavy; food helps, tablets treat; what the doctor checks; managing a heavy day; heavy periods can be treated |
| **The days before** | Calm breath (2 minutes, then 5) | The calm week, the day's note, a calcium food | Is it PMS; notice, name, pause; stress; catch the thought; anger; plan the hard week; sleep; when to get more help |

What it never does:
- it never promises easier, lighter or calmer periods, and never says food lightens bleeding or eases PMS by itself;
- it never names or doses a painkiller, an iron tablet or a hormone. Her doctor chooses them; the plan helps her ask;
- it never says "endometriosis", "fibroids" or "PMDD" about her. Only a doctor can name a cause. A doctor's own diagnosis opens
  that version's swaps.

## 3. How it is personalised: what she tells Sakhi, and what changes

| What Sakhi knows | Where it comes from | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| Her period goals (up to 3) | Joining's goals (39.10), or My plan | Which goal leads, and which join in their windows |
| Her last periods | Joining's dates (39.26), or her diary | Her likely start, so the windows land on the right days. Irregular or no dates: the plan counts weeks and her taps anchor it ("My period started", "My hard days have started") |
| Pain that stops her day, heavy flow | Joining's two ticks (39.26) | A doctor card this week, from day 1. Heavy flow: the Hb test leads |
| Hormones | Joining's pill question | On the pill, the bleed counts as her period. New mood changes since starting a method: "tell the doctor who prescribed it" |
| Her age | Joining's year of birth | 40 and over with heavy periods: the reviewed bleeding-changes card pinned once. 45 and over: bleeding between periods is a "see a doctor" sign |
| A doctor's diagnosis | Only her answer to "Has a doctor told you…?" in the plan's own questions | Endometriosis or adenomyosis: 4 practice days, a flare plan, the pain diary (`cond-endo`). Fibroids: the fibroid card |
| Tired, dizzy or breathless | One question in the heavy version's own questions | Walks wait until the Hb test or her "I feel OK to walk"; strength waits longer |
| Where she gets care | Joining's "Where do you usually go?" (39.28) | "Your doctor" by default; the free government test or OPD as one quiet line, or first for a woman who picks government care |
| Her day, food and body | Joining's details, My food, her knees or back | Where moving starts; iron and calcium foods by region and diet; swaps for knees or back |
| A shared phone | Discreet mode | Neutral titles and reminders; "Pain today?" becomes "How was today?"; private steps hidden |

The plan's own questions add only what it uses: the doctor's diagnosis (1 question), and for heavy, "Often tired, dizzy or
breathless?" and "Has a doctor given you an iron tablet?". For PMS: her signs, up to 3 of the 9, and how much they get in the
way. Everything else comes from joining.

## 4. The minimalist baseline, applied

- **Day 1: 3 rows at most:**
  - today's practice (its short version);
  - moving, or for heavy the Hb test;
  - her window's step, only when she's in a window.
  This week's chapter is one row under them, as in the midlife plan. Strength joins from week 5 on 2 of her moving days, as the
  4th row at most. On a period day the kit or the rest routine takes moving's place.
- **Her evening:** one check-in at her time, after her walk (39.27). Her measure sits inside it on window days only. No morning
  ring.
- **Rings:**
  - the check-in;
  - her iron tablet at its own time, only if her doctor gave one (`iron.js` already rings);
  - no new ring. "Get your kit ready" and "Your period is likely in 2 days" are Today rows.
- **Ask less:** dates, ticks, goals, hormones, food and care come from joining. The plan's own questions are 1 to 3 short ones.
- **One home for each fact:**
  - the period-day measure is in the check-in, not a second card;
  - the PMS log's own Today row hides while the plan's note runs;
  - the doctor cards come one at a time;
  - the iron row on Today steps aside while the plan shows the tablet.
- **Explanation folds after week 1;** her steps, tools and settings stay open.
- **No new tours, tips or celebrations.** A missed day is never a broken streak. A hard period brings a lighter week.
- **The shared-phone check:** neutral titles ("Today's step", "How was today?"), the kit and the note hidden behind her PIN,
  and the crisis line working in discreet mode.
- **Measure it:** taps for joining and the everyday jobs before and after, as each build does.

## 5. Safety first: what must exist before the plan

1. **A late period comes first.** Before any kit step, on any day after her likely range has ended without a period, or more
   than 35 days since her last start, Today shows "Period late?" (39.25, 39.27) with one-sided pain on top. The kit opens only
   after "Period came" or "Not pregnant". Pregnancy pauses the plan (existing `life.pause`).
2. **Pain signs that need a doctor** (the bank, `periods-pain`'s "Not normal" list):
   - pain that stops her day, or that keeps getting worse;
   - pain that started after years of easy periods;
   - pain with sex, bleeding between periods or after sex, a fever or unusual discharge;
   - bowel or urine pain at period time;
   - one-sided pain with a late period or a positive test: Call 112 now (Help's own line).
3. **Bleeding signs:**
   - soaking a pad or cloth every hour for 2 hours or more, or bleeding with dizziness or fainting: today, or Call 112;
   - clots bigger than a ₹10 coin, or changing at night: a doctor this week;
   - bleeding between periods or after sex: a doctor;
   - heavy bleeding on blood-thinning medicine: today.
4. **Her Hb.** A fixed line beside every Hb she enters, the same for everyone: "If your report or doctor says your Hb is below
   8, see a doctor this week; today if you're breathless, dizzy or have chest pain." WHO's 2024 guideline keeps severe anaemia
   below 8 g/dL for non-pregnant women. New wording, so the gynaecologist confirms it.
5. **PMS and low mood:**
   - "Need help now?" on every calm-week screen opens Help's crisis sheet: Tele-MANAS 14416, 112, and 181 if home isn't safe;
   - low most of the month, or thoughts of harm, is not PMS (the chapter says so);
   - Version 39.30's urgent card already catches "thoughts of harm" typed in search.
   One active question ("In the last 2 weeks, have you had thoughts of harming yourself?"), at the reveal and once each calm
   week, waits for a psychologist's review. Until then only "Need help now?" ships.
6. **Pauses:** in pregnancy; for a year after a birth, or until her periods come back; after a loss until her first period;
   when her periods stop (a doctor card; bleeding after periods stop is a "see a doctor" sign).
7. **Medicines:** "Ask a doctor or chemist which painkiller and when, never someone else's." Ask first with a stomach ulcer,
   asthma, kidney, liver or heart disease, or if you could be pregnant. Black stools or vomiting blood: hospital now. No names
   or doses (the 24 Oct lesson 6, reviewed then for wording).

## 6. Where a doctor comes in

The plan is her first step for what habits can change. For anything beyond it, she is pointed to a doctor:
- **A Qura doctor** (a free call) for help beyond the plan:
  - the first look at her periods and her diary;
  - tests (Hb, thyroid, a scan when needed);
  - food, weight, movement, sleep and cycle care;
  - a referral to an MBBS gynaecologist when she needs medicines.
  Qura's own reviewed lines say this already.
- **An MBBS gynaecologist** for the right painkiller, tablets that reduce bleeding, hormone treatment and PMDD medicines.
- **Urgent signs, any day:** Help's own lines, "Call 112 now" first.

The cards, one at a time:
- **At the start:** her ticks or answers ("It stops my day"; soaking within 1 to 2 hours or ₹10-coin clots; PMS "a lot") bring
  the reviewed doctor line this week. A "Doctor visit: booked / been" row follows to week 4.
- **Week 4, for everyone:** "When to see a doctor", beside her own facts.
- **Her second period (PMS):** "If the week before your period stops you working, studying or caring for your family in most
  cycles, see a doctor within 2 weeks; take your note."
- **After 3 cycles, for everyone:** "Still hard? A doctor can treat painful and heavy periods and PMS. Take your record."

Every card's first button is "Ask a Qura doctor about this", and Version 39.53's row puts this plan first on the period guides.

## 7. Her measure and results

All numbers are her own, period by period, from the check-in, so she never enters anything twice:
- **Pain:** the worst pain each period (the diary's 4 levels), the days she couldn't do her day, the painkiller days.
- **Heavy:**
  - the days heavy bleeding got in her way each period (leaked, changed at night, couldn't do her day), and her bleeding days;
  - her Hb exactly as she typed it, with her report's range: "your doctor says what this means".
- **PMS:** the share of her noted days with her signs in the 7 days before her period, and the "a lot" days. Cycle days 4 to 10
  appear only in the report for her doctor, never as a two-bar gap that could read as a diagnosis.

Results compare her 3 periods before the plan (from her diary, or what she told Sakhi) with the 3 since. Every card says
"Periods vary from month to month, and what you take can change them; look at several." It never says "improved".

## 9. Decisions for Mohit (with recommendations)

1. **One plan or three?** One plan, "My period plan", with her first goal leading (recommended). "Easier periods" and "Stronger
   blood" can read as promises (the 24 Oct lawyer question).
2. **Routines:** reuse the existing routines in version 1 (recommended), or build Unwind, Period yoga and Rest and restore now
   (a physiotherapist, 3D poses, recordings).
3. **A calcium food for PMS:** ACOG's 2023 guideline conditionally recommends calcium for PMS (low-quality evidence). Food only,
   no tablet or dose, as the midlife plan's calcium food (recommended, for the doctor's review), or nothing on food.
4. **The Hb line at "below 8":** WHO 2024's severe band. For the gynaecologist.
5. **The PMS safety question:** ship only "Need help now?" until a psychologist reviews the active question (recommended).
6. **Heavy periods at 40 and over, still bleeding:** this plan's heavy version with the bleeding-changes card (recommended), not
   the midlife plan.
7. **The endometriosis swaps** (4 practice days, a flare plan, the pain diary), only after a doctor's diagnosis: in version 1
   (recommended), or later.
8. **Women on the three old plans:** keep them, with one offer to move (recommended).
9. **Rings:** only the check-in and her doctor's tablet. The kit and "likely in 2 days" are Today rows (recommended).
