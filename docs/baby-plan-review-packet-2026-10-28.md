## Evidence behind the plan

From `docs/plans-design-and-build-2026-10-24-programmes.md`, section 8.

- Folic acid from before conception to 12 weeks (WHO, through Sakhi’s verified articles). Neural tube defects: about 4.5 per 1,000 births in India (Allagh 2015).
- Anemia Mukt Bharat: switch to folic acid when planning a pregnancy and continue to 12 weeks; ANMs give it at village health and nutrition days.
- Pregnancy often comes quickly: 38% in the first cycle and 81% by the sixth in a German cohort (Gnoth 2003). Used for the design only, not shown to her.
- Coaching the gaps improves habits: diet risk scores improved with coaching (van Dijk 2020), with no effect on pregnancy shown.
- Pooled lifestyle trials before conception: no effect on pregnancy or live birth; 10 or more sessions was only an exploratory subgroup (Torkel 2026). So the plan promises readiness and health, never a baby.
- MMNP: the food effect showed only when started 90 or more days before conception, per protocol; the intention-to-treat result was null.
- WINGS: Hb gains before pregnancy faded unless continued in pregnancy.
- Lifestyle programmes before fertility treatment did not improve births (Mutsaerts 2016; Legro 2022).
- Vigorous activity was linked to lower fecundability in lean women (Wise 2012, observational).
- The waiting-week coping card rests on one IVF feasibility study (Lancastle and Boivin 2008); the later trial was not verified.
- When to get checked (12 months under 35, 6 at 35 to 39, straight away at 40 or over and with the listed conditions): the verified card on the tests page.
- The PMOS plan’s activity numbers: the 2023 international PCOS guideline (150 to 300 minutes a week, 250 for modest weight loss, strength on 2 days).
