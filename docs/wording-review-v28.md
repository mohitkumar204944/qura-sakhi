# New wording in Version 28, for the doctor to check (22 Oct 2026)

Version 28 adds four features:
- **Iron tablets:** a daily tick, a reminder and help.
- **Free check-ups for you:** a Home card for women aged 30 to 65.
- **My ASHA or ANM:** her number, a one-tap call and a summary to show her.
- **Do it online:** the government's own websites.

Mohit's rule is that new health wording needs a doctor's review before it ships. So this file lists every new sentence a
woman will read, in English and Hindi. Lines already verified on 20 Oct are reused word for word, and section 2 names where
each one comes from. Only sections 1 and 3 need a fresh look.

- **Birth costs:** PIB's NSS 80th round note (January to December 2025), table B3-II. The average out-of-pocket spend per

**A conflict to settle (Mohit or the doctor).** The verified PMMVY text says the first ₹3,000 comes "after at least one
antenatal check-up". So does the verified Learn article on schemes, which cites the ministry's FAQ. The FAQ says this in one
answer, but its conditions answer says "Registration of Pregnancy and at least two Ante-Natal Check-up (ANC) preferably within
six months from LMP".
- **What the app does now:** the new "apply now" line waits for two check-ups, which is true under either reading.
- **If two is right:** the verified PMMVY text and the article should change, for example: "₹3,000 after the pregnancy is
  registered and you have had two antenatal check-ups, best within six months of your last period".
- **If one is right:** the line can show after one.
