# Sakhi: whole-app audit, 22 Oct 2026

Version 26 (APK 0.25-demo). Mohit asked: "can you audit what scope of improvement we have with app".

How this was done:

- Every screen was measured in Chromium at Android phone size, in English, Hindi and discreet mode.
- Startup was timed at normal, 4x and 6x slower CPU.
- Two code reviews ran alongside: the Android wrapper, and the content and translations. Their serious claims were checked
  against the code before going in here.
- Product gates respected:
  - no fertile or safe days;
  - no automated condition alerts until CDSCO decides;
  - no diary data to Meta;
  - 18+;
  - reproductive health only;
  - no competitor walkthroughs;
  - health wording changes need doctor review.

## Verdict

The app itself is in good shape:

- All 118 screens render cleanly.
- It starts fast even on a slow phone.
- Home, Diary, Care and Help are polished and bilingual.
- The privacy design is strong (allowlisted network fields, no cloud backup, discreet mode, lock).

What stands between it and real women is mostly trust and plumbing, not UX polish:

- two help features that say a message was sent when nothing was;
- three ways a shared phone can still reveal the diary;
- a throwaway signing key, and no way to update a sideloaded app;
- legal placeholders.

After those come growth items: the doctor hand-off to Qura, feedback, and knowing what is used.

## P0: before any real woman uses it

1. **Help that goes nowhere says it was sent** (S to M).
   - "Speak to a doctor" is the main button on Help. It saves the request on the phone and shows "Request saved. Reference
     QC-1234". Nothing reaches Qura.
   - "Tell a person" promises "A person at Qura reads this… and replies within 1 working day" and then says "Received". It
     too stays on the phone (`app.js` renderAsk / renderTell).
   - Fix now: honest words, and a direct call or WhatsApp link to Qura's team.
   - Fix properly: the doctor hand-off in SERVER_SPEC 10.1, which is also Qura's consult funnel.
2. **The shared phone still leaks** (S each). Checked in `android/src`:
   - The recents screen shows her last diary screen: no FLAG_SECURE or `setRecentsScreenshotEnabled`.
   - Sakhi's lock also accepts the phone's own PIN or any enrolled finger or face (`BIOMETRIC_WEAK | DEVICE_CREDENTIAL`,
     MainActivity:249), and the prompt opens by itself. The person holding her phone usually knows the phone PIN.
   - Notifications say "Qura Sakhi" (the application label), even in discreet mode. The heads-up text shows on the lock
     screen: there's no `setVisibility` or `setPublicVersion`. The channel description says "period reminders".
3. **The signing key** (S). The APK is signed with the prototype key (password "android"). Copies can only ever be updated
   with the same key; switching later means uninstalling, which erases her diary. Make the real key before the first real
   install.
4. **No way to update a sideloaded app** (M). Nothing checks for a newer version. Ship a daily "new version available"
   check (a small static file listing the latest and minimum version) in the first real build.
5. **Legal placeholders** (S, needs Mohit):
   - "Grievance Officer: [NAME, ADDRESS]";
   - the notice "The live app will name every processor, the retention period…";
   - no privacy policy URL (Play also needs one, plus a web page for account deletion).
6. **"Needs approval" features are visible to women** (decision). On 8 module home screens, gated features (CDSCO and
   doctor sign-off) show with a "Needs approval" tag even with prototype labels off. For real women: hide them, or show a
   plain "coming later". Don't leave them half-offered.

## P1: in the first weeks

7. **Google Fonts on every launch** (S). Seven families load from Google each time. That tells Google her IP, contradicts
   "nothing is sent", and fails offline. Bundle the few fonts used.
8. **Health text can leave through WhatsApp or the read-aloud voice** (S).
   - The doctor report is shared as a `wa.me/?text=` link (app.js:2061): through the browser if WhatsApp doesn't catch it.
     Use Android's share sheet instead.
   - Read-aloud may use a network voice on some phones. Prefer on-phone voices.
9. **A WebView crash closes the app** (S). Handle `onRenderProcessGone`. Also restore after a failed state restore.
10. **Reminders go quiet** (S).
    - Only 21 days or 60 alarms are queued, refilled when she opens the app.
    - On Xiaomi, Oppo and Vivo, battery savers block the after-restart re-scheduling.
    - Add a last "open Sakhi" alarm and a phone-brand help card.
11. **Large text** (S to M). Set WebView text zoom from the phone's font size explicitly, and test at 200%. Fix the 9–10 px
    text on four module screens.
12. **The crash fallback** (S). "This screen hit an error in the prototype" is English-only (even in Hindi) and says
    "prototype". Make it friendly, bilingual, with a way back. Guard module code against a malformed diary from an old
    backup.
13. **The build can quietly ship the stand-in server** (S). Refuse to build a non-demo APK without an https server address.
14. **Safety net for the accounts database** (M). The free plan has no reliable backups and the weekly export is manual.
    Automate a daily encrypted export, and keep the project awake (it pauses after 7 quiet days).
15. **CI and lost tests** (M). Run `npm test` on every push (GitHub Actions). Rebuild a walk of the main flows: first run,
    logging a period, check-in, backup and restore, lock, discreet mode.
16. **Feedback and sharing** (M). A "Send feedback" and a "Share Sakhi" row (Me audit item l). These are what turn testers
    into a feedback loop.
17. **Internal notes in article sources** (S, confirm with the doctor reviewer). 27 of 69 articles cite an internal file
    ("via docs/_product-vision-womens-health-companion-oct18.md §…"), and about 28 mention a failed fetch ("returned 403").
    Read-aloud speaks them too. Remove the internal lines; the health wording doesn't change.
18. **Read-aloud** (S to M):
    - It reads collapsed sections, including those sources.
    - Pop-up sheets can't be read aloud: the emergency numbers, the "what will be shared" consent, the privacy notice, the
      day log. Only quick answers have Listen.
    - Fix: skip closed sections, and make the top-bar speaker read the open sheet first.
19. **Extra languages fall back badly** (S).
    - Learn articles show in English for Marathi readers, though the picker promises "the rest in Hindi"
      (`L()` at app.js:2202 skips the Hindi fallback).
    - The emergency-numbers sheet is English for Marathi, Bengali, Tamil and Telugu (app.js:1620), though translated labels
      already exist in lang.js.
20. **Urgent guidance buried in long sentences** (M, needs doctor review). 103 English sentences run over 30 words. Seven of
    the ten longest are colon lists; one carries the ectopic-pregnancy warning in 46 words. Show such lists as bullets with
    the same words.

## P2: later, or for Play

21. **Long screens** (M to L). 15 screens are over 4 phone screens. Split them, or fold detail behind "More"; teen-mother
    and intimate-safety first.
22. **Startup weight** (M). Load modules when first opened instead of all 23 at start. Fine on mid-range phones today; it
    matters on the cheapest ones.
23. **Backup code strength** (S to M). AES-GCM with PBKDF2 at 150k rounds, but a 4-digit PIN is only 10,000 guesses, minutes
    to crack. Ask for 6+ digits, or a word phrase, for backups.
24. **The first run** (M). About 10 screens before Home. Merge decade and year into one picker, and ask "who uses this
    phone" later. Signup for all stays (Mohit's decision).
25. **Knowing what's used** (decision, then M). Qura sees signups, active days, district or state and "heard from". Feature
    use is invisible. If wanted: opt-in counts of screens opened, not linked to the account. That's a privacy decision
    first.
26. **A Qura stats view** (M). Signups and active users by day and state, today read from the Supabase Table Editor.
27. **Play readiness** (M to L), when distribution is decided:
    - targetSdk 36 (Play requires it for new apps and updates since 31 Aug 2026), with edge-to-edge and predictive back;
    - an AAB (possible without Gradle, via bundletool, which is a download);
    - the Data safety form, the health apps declaration and the age audience;
    - Google's developer verification (global in 2027; Trinaya will need a D-U-N-S number).
28. **Smaller Android hardening** (S): sensitive clipboard flag, remove unused WebView settings, a Content-Security-Policy,
    crash reporting.

29. **More languages** (L, any new text needs doctor review). Marathi, Bengali, Tamil and Telugu cover 17.6% of core UI and no
    module text; the picker says "partly". If extended, start with the danger signs and Help.
30. **Content checks** (S).
    - Add a test for article links in the Home question tables, danger signs and journey; today a missing article fails
      silently.
    - Agree one script per term in a Hindi glossary (test/टेस्ट, pad/पैड, reminder/रिमाइंडर).
    - Read SVG labels aloud.

## Already strong

- **The diary never leaves the phone:**
  - `net()` sends only the allowlisted fields;
  - no cloud backup (`allowBackup=false`);
  - no cleartext http;
  - no Java logs of personal data.
- **The Android bridge** returns no diary data, and outside pages can't reach it.
- **Signup and server:** WhatsApp with SMS fallback works live. OTPs are hashed and rate-limited. Delete removes the
  account.
- **Plain, bilingual and safe:**
  - all 69 articles fully in Hindi, with no broken links and an average reading grade of 6.7;
  - "no safe days" stated on Home and Diary;
  - emergency signs and 112 on Help;
  - self-harm line 14416;
  - read-aloud;
  - questions in women's own words ("safed pani", "PCOD").
- **Engagement that respects privacy:** the heads-up card before a period, petals and the monthly flower, Today's card, the
  monthly check-in, the full tour.
- **Fast:** the slow-phone startup is about half a second.

## Suggested order

- **Week 1 (P0, mostly small):**
  - honest wording on the two help features, plus a call or WhatsApp link;
  - the three shared-phone leaks;
  - the real signing key;
  - the update check;
  - the legal text, once Mohit sends the details;
  - hide the gated features.
- **Weeks 2–3 (P1):** bundle the fonts, the share sheet, crash recovery, reminders, large text, the friendly crash screen,
  database backups, CI and a regression walk, feedback and sharing, the article sources, read-aloud and the language
  fallbacks.
- **Then:** the doctor hand-off (SERVER_SPEC 10.1), long screens, lazy loading, and Play readiness once distribution is
  decided.
