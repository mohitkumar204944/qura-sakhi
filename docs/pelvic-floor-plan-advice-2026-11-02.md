## 1. The short answer

- **One plan, "My pelvic floor plan"** (`pelvic` version 1), for a woman who leaks urine (with a cough, sneeze, laugh or lift,
  or on the way to the toilet) or feels heaviness down below.
- **Two tracks:**
  - leaks (stress, urge or both): 12 weeks;
  - heaviness: a doctor first. Until then, only finding the muscles and lying sets, for up to 4 weeks. After "a doctor said
    prolapse", 16 weeks.
  Both then keep going to month 12 ("Keep strong": one set a day, or sets on 3 days).
- **Every day:**
  - her squeeze sets, 3 a day, in 8 levels that unlock from her sets, never the calendar;
  - one small habit that changes every 2 weeks (the knack, water, the toilet, lifting, then sets during her day);
  - on the urge track, "When the urge comes".
  Day 1 has 2 steps, and no day more than 3.
- **One safety screen** before her first set. The signs NICE sends straight to a doctor must not wait 3 months of exercises.
- **A chapter a week**, and one card at a time, with a Qura doctor first for what the plan can't do.
- **Her own measure, in her own words:** "Did you leak this week?", how much it gets in the way, and whether she is sure she is
  squeezing the right way.
- **Pregnancy and after a birth keep the pelvic floor steps they already have.** As one of her goals beside another plan (My
  period plan, My midlife plan, Getting ready), her sets join that plan as one step.
- **Most of it exists:**
  - the pelvic floor trainer (`post-pelvic`: holds, sets, the 3-month course);
  - the reviewed guide "Prolapse and leaking urine" (`intimate-prolapse`);
  - the midlife guide's leak lines;
  - the after-birth plan's weekly leaks question;
  - the 3D stills for the positions.
  New: the plan, its levels, the safety screen, the knack cards, about 16 chapters, and a few pictures.

## 2. Why this plan: what the evidence says

**It is common, and few women ask for help.**
- An Ahmedabad study in urban slums (476 women): 30% leaked urine, and only 7% of them had seen a doctor.
- A Lucknow hospital survey (3,000 women): 21.8% leaked. Three in four of them had stress leaks (with a cough, sneeze or
  lift); mixed leaks were the most common kind in Ahmedabad.
- Sakhi's own guide already says it plainly: "It is not 'just age', you don't have to live with it, and it can be treated."

**Exercise works, if it is done properly and long enough.**
- NICE NG123 (1.4.4 to 1.4.5): first treatment is supervised pelvic floor muscle training for at least 3 months, "at least 8
  contractions performed 3 times per day". NG210 (1.6.14) says the same for stress or mixed leaks, pregnant women included.
- Cochrane 2018: stress leaks were cured in 56% of women who did the exercises, against 6% who didn't (RR 8.38, high-quality
  evidence).
- Urge and mixed leaks: bladder training for at least 6 weeks is first-line (NG123), with a trial of less caffeine when the
  bladder is overactive, and weight loss when the BMI is over 30. With mixed leaks, treat the main symptom first.
- Prolapse: at least 16 weeks of supervised training for a mild prolapse (NG123 1.7.5), and at least 4 months if it is no more
  than 1 cm past the opening (NG210 1.6.13). Exercise can ease heaviness, but it doesn't put things back.

**An app can carry the course; a person should check the squeeze.**
- The Tät app trial (Sweden, n=123): leak scores improved 3.2 points more than in the waiting group at 3 months. Sjöström's
  internet programme did as well as the postal one, and the gains held for 2 years.
- An Indian trial (PGIMER, n=140): one-to-one training plus a self-help manual beat the manual alone. So Sakhi is the manual
  plus the reminders, and it sends her to have her squeeze checked.
- NICE asks for supervised training. Sakhi can't examine her, so the plan asks in week 1, 4 and 12 whether she is sure, and
  "Is it the right squeeze?" leads to a doctor or physiotherapist.

**What it must never do:** diagnose a prolapse, a fistula, an infection or a nerve problem. Those go to a doctor, through the
same signs as the guide.

## 3. Who it is for, and where it fits

Women 18 and over. As at joining, girls under 18 get no plan.

| Her stage | What she gets |
|---|---|
| Tracking my periods | A new goal tile, "Leaking urine or heaviness". It leads when it is her first goal. Otherwise her sets join My period plan |
| Trying for a baby | Her sets join Getting ready as one step. Getting ready stays her plan (the 24 Oct design: "an add-on only") |
| Pregnant | Unchanged: My pregnancy has its pelvic floor step from week 13. A leak that may be fluid from the vagina goes to the waters line first |
| After a birth | Unchanged: My recovery plan has its squeezes and its weekly leaks question to the first birthday |
| Periods changing after 40, periods stopped | The same tile. It leads, or her sets join My midlife plan. Moves are bone-safe by default |

- **When it joins another plan:** her sets are one step there, "Your squeeze sets", 3 a day. The plan's habits come as the
  chapter's "Try this week" instead of extra steps. On a day that already has 4 steps, the sets show as the one row under her
  steps until they are done.
- **When her stage changes:**
  - a plan from another stage gets the "may fit now" card, as in 39.48 and 39.55;
  - into a pregnancy, this plan pauses: "Your plan has changed", and My pregnancy's pelvic floor step carries on;
  - after a birth, My recovery plan takes over, with its own squeezes and leaks question.

## 4. The plan: tracks, weeks and days

**Her track, from one question:** "When do you leak?"
- With a cough, sneeze, laugh or lift: the stress track.
- On the way to the toilet, or a sudden strong need: the urge track.
- Both.
- I feel heaviness or a bulge: the heaviness track.
- Not sure: the stress track, with the urge step added when she taps "rushing" in a look-back.

**The weeks** (from the 24 Oct design):

| Weeks | Part | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 2 | Find it | The right squeeze and a full let-go, the mistakes, level 1 lying; the knack from week 2 |
| 3 to 4 | Build the hold | Longer holds as levels unlock, quick squeezes, water through the day; the 4-week check |
| 5 to 6 | Less pushing down | Knees up on the toilet, breathe out, no hard pushing; urge: the gap between trips grows |
| 7 to 8 | Standing and lifting | Standing sets when her level reaches them; lifting the safe way; the halfway look-back |
| 9 to 12 | Into your day | Sets during tasks, the warning-signs lesson, her keep-strong plan, results |
| 13 to 16 (heaviness, after a doctor) | Steady | A rest at the end of the day; the ring pessary and sex chapters |
| To month 12 | Keep strong | One set a day, or sets on 3 days; a recap a month |

**Today, at most 3 steps:**
1. **Your squeeze sets**, 3 a day at her times. The trainer opens with a play button (39.55's rule), and finishing a set counts
   it. "I did my sets" in the check-in counts sets done without the phone.
2. **One small habit**, changing every 2 weeks: the knack (week 2), water through the day (weeks 3 to 4), on the toilet
   (weeks 5 to 6), lifting the safe way (weeks 7 to 8), one set standing at a task (weeks 9 to 12). The urge track adds
   "When the urge comes: stop, squeeze, breathe, walk calmly", and from week 3 "wait a little longer" each week.
3. **A check on its days:** "Find the right muscles" on days 1 to 3, and on the first day of a new position; the 4-week check;
   "Get checked once" on the heaviness track until she has.

Day 1 has her first set and "Find the right muscles": 2 steps.

**The levels** (8, as designed on 24 Oct): from lying, 3-second holds ×8, to standing and walking, 10-second holds ×10 with
10 quick squeezes.
- A level unlocks after enough sets at the one before, never by the date.
- After 3 weeks away, she steps back one level.
- Pain during a set twice switches her to "Breathe and let go", with a doctor card.
- On period days, a lying set counts, and one set is enough.

## 5. Safety first: the one new screen

"Before you start", a few taps. Nothing has to be answered, and "None of these" is a tap.

| Her tap | What Sakhi does |
|---|---|
| I can't pass urine, or it dribbles all the time | See a doctor today (overflow) |
| Leaking all the time, day and night, after a long labour or an operation | See a doctor this week. A fistula can be repaired, free at government hospitals, and must not wait 3 months of exercises |
| Burning, fever, or blood in the urine | See a doctor (an infection is not a weak pelvic floor: the guide's own line) |
| A bulge that stays outside | Today; now if it is very painful (the guide's lines) |
| Wind or stool I can't hold | See a doctor this week |
| Pain when I squeeze, or pain with sex | "Breathe and let go" instead of squeezes, and a doctor card |
| A past operation for leaks or prolapse (mesh), or cancer treatment in the pelvis | "Show this plan to your doctor first". Levels stay at 2 until she says a doctor or physiotherapist said OK |

At any time, in Help and the guide: sudden loss of bladder or bowel control with numbness between the legs, weak legs or bad back
pain means call 112 now. This is a new line for the doctor.

The reason for a new screen before her first step (the minimalist baseline asks for one): NICE NG123 1.3.20 sends these
straight to a doctor, and in India constant leaking after a long labour can be a fistula.

## 6. Where a doctor comes in: a Qura doctor first

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Start | Her taps on the safety screen, as above |
| Heaviness, week 1 until done | "Get checked once: a doctor names the cause, not the app". It opens the guide's "Where to go", Care near me and Ask a Qura doctor |
| Weeks 2 to 4, and whenever she taps "not sure" | **Is it the right squeeze?** A Qura doctor first, then her own doctor or a physiotherapist, who can check it. The free government option is one quiet line (39.28). This replaces the 24 Oct design's "free squeeze check" at the PHC |
| Week 12 (16), month 12 | **Still leaking or heavy?** "A doctor can treat this", with Ask a Qura doctor first. Then the guide's treatment ladder: a ring pessary, medicines for an urgent bladder (her doctor decides), a treatment inside the vagina after menopause (her doctor decides), surgery |
| 40 and over | Bleeding after menopause: within 2 weeks (the existing line) |
| A ring pessary in place, with bleeding, a bad smell, pain, or a check overdue | This week (a fixed card) |
| 2 infections in 6 months | See a doctor |

A one-line crisis link sits in chapter 1 and in her results: "Feeling low or alone because of this? Tele-MANAS 14416 is free."

## 7. Her evening, her measure and her results

- **Her evening:**
  - the check-in after her last set, at bedtime, as every plan's;
  - "I did my sets" for sets done away from the phone;
  - on the urge track in weeks 1 and 7, an optional count of toilet trips on the My measure tile, never on Today.
- **The main measure, weekly:** "Did you leak this week? Never / sometimes / most days". It reuses the after-birth plan's
  reviewed question, and the heaviness track asks the same about heaviness.
- **Now and then:**
  - "How much does it get in the way of your day?" (0 to 3), at the start and in weeks 4, 8 and 12;
  - "Are you sure you're squeezing the right way?", in weeks 1, 4 and 12;
  - "Compared with when you started: better, the same, or worse?", at week 12.
- **Her results:**
  - leaks, her first 2 weeks against her last 2;
  - "gets in the way", at the start against week 12;
  - set days and the level she reached;
  - "sure", week 1 against week 12.
  Beside them, the same card for everyone: still leaking after 3 months, see a doctor. It never says "cured".
- **In our own words:** ICIQ-UI SF needs a paid licence for an app (the 19 Oct check), and POP-SS's licence is unclear. A
  reviewer compares our wording with ICIQ-UI SF's items 3 and 4.

## 8. The minimalist baseline, applied

- **Her evening.** The check-in rings, as every plan's. The sets need reminders: NICE asks for 3 a day for 3 months, and
  sticking with it is where training fails. So, by default:
  - her morning set rings at her time (new; its reason is the 3-a-day dose);
  - her bedtime set is named on the check-in's ring ("Your last set, then 1 minute"), so it adds no ring;
  - her midday set rides on lunch, and the morning ring's words name it.
  Off in Me stays off.
- **Today:** day 1 has 2 steps and no day more than 3; one Sakhi says card; one row under her steps.
- **Ask less:** the goal tile, the safety screen (its reason above) and "When do you leak?". Her set times come from the plan's
  "When can you move for 10 minutes?" when she has answered it, and the trainer's own settings. Nothing else is asked.
- **One home for each fact:**
  - the trainer is the sets' only home;
  - the guide holds the treatments;
  - the check-in holds the measure;
  - the knack is a card after a set, not a step of its own.
- **Read once folds after week 1.** No new tours, tips or celebrations, and a missed week steps her back a level, never "start
  again".
- **The shared phone:**
  - the title is "My plan" and the sets are "Your 2 minutes";
  - the reminders' words are neutral, with no leak words anywhere;
  - the count uses tones, vibration and big numbers, because read-aloud is off on a shared phone;
  - the chapters are behind her PIN.
- **Taps** are measured before and after, as for every plan.

## 10. Decisions for Mohit (with my recommendations)

1. **One plan with two tracks**: leaks for 12 weeks; heaviness with a doctor first, then 16 weeks; both keep going to month 12.
   Recommended: yes.
2. **Where it applies:**
   - it leads at the tracking, midlife and stopped stages;
   - it joins as one step beside My period plan, My midlife plan and Getting ready;
   - pregnancy and after a birth stay as they are.
   Recommended: yes.
3. **Rings:** the morning set rings, and the bedtime set rides on the check-in's ring (one new ring). The alternatives are 3
   rings (one per set) or none. Recommended: one new ring.
4. **"Is it the right squeeze?"** A Qura doctor first, then her doctor or a physiotherapist, with the free government option as
   a quiet line, as the doctor-first rule says. Recommended: yes.
5. **Tea and coffee:** "less tea, coffee and cola for 2 weeks" on the urge track only (NICE: a trial of less caffeine for an
   overactive bladder), never as a general rule. This was left open on 24 Oct. Recommended: yes.
6. **The count:** tones, vibration and big numbers by default, with Dr. Megha's voice as an option (new clips, made the way
   Wind down's were). Recommended: yes.
7. **Pictures:**
   - now: the existing 3D stills for the positions, and a 2D sling;
   - after the physiotherapist reviews them: the new 3D poses.
   Recommended: yes.
8. **Reviewers:** the doctor for every new line and the 112 line, and a women's-health physiotherapist for the levels,
   positions and routines. As with the other plans, the build need not wait, but nothing ships to pilot women until they have
   signed. Recommended: yes.
9. **The measure in our own words**, not ICIQ-UI SF (a paid licence). Look again at the licence for the field-test read-out.
   Recommended: yes.
10. **The name:** "My pelvic floor plan" (Hindi "मेरा पेल्विक फ़्लोर प्लान"), and the goal tile "Leaking urine or
    heaviness" ("पेशाब का रिसाव या भारीपन"). It follows My period plan and My midlife plan. Recommended: yes.
