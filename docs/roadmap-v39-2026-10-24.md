## 0. The answer on one page

**On the plans docs: adopt the architecture, with changes. None of the 8 programmes is ready to build yet, and none
belongs in Version 39.**
- **What's strong:**
  - each programme is a pure-data file run by one engine;
  - a golden test proves the port changes nothing before anything new is added;
  - her health entries may only *offer* a change, never make one;
  - inside a programme, doctor cards are the same for every woman;
  - a safety layer that works without any programme;
  - at most 3 rows and 1 card on Today;
  - a short core, then a long light phase.

  This is the right skeleton, and it answers CDSCO's line on triage cleanly.
- **What has to change first:**
  - **The safety layer is ordered after a 3 to 5 week port, and its discreet fix names the wrong lines.** The real hiding
    place is core (`app.js:1831`), and three of the safety items are live harms in today's build.
  - **The red-flag bank isn't yet a safe single source.** About 15 rows contradict reviewed lines. Bleeding after a birth
    is set at "2 pads an hour" where reviewed lines say 1. Chest pain gets three different urgencies. Fever is missing from
    the pregnancy set, and post-partum pre-eclampsia from the after-birth set.
  - **Care after a loss would store the kind of loss** ("abortion", "ectopic") on a shared phone, which reverses
    `loss.js`'s promise to store nothing.
  - **The programmes break their own main rule in places:** pain-level and "harder" taps driving doctor cards, a dizzy chip
    that gates steps silently, a PHQ-9-style safety question, a 0-to-10 flush rating that mirrors a licensed scale.
  - **Scope creep:** 22,600 new words, about 28 poses, roughly 6 new Qura offer points, a "where I'm staying" switch,
    testicle lines, and TB and thyroid lines in Midlife. After the review's cuts it's about 17,000 words.
  - **Gates that cost money or can be faked:** paid reviewers and studies as hard gates, and a "signed record" that any
    session could write.

**The merged order:**
1. **V39.0, fix and protect, now.** The P0s, the iron bug and the live safety slips, using reviewed lines only.
2. **V39.1 to V39.3:** one home for what her records say, fewer places to look, and food and search in her words.
3. **The field test** on a frozen 0.39.3-pilot build.
4. **V39.4 to V39.9, while it runs:** the engine port (nothing visible changes), one plan page, results and the doctor
   loop, knowledge, pictures, and food steps that change.
5. **V40, the safety layer for every woman:** the crisis sheet, care after a loss, and the reconciled red-flag bank.
6. **V41 onward, one programme at a time,** smallest first, with the food track (meals, then recipes) alongside.

**What sets the calendar is review, not code.** Sections 13 to 16 still wait, and the plan adds §17 to §28. The
gynaecologist and Dr. Megha each need a weekly slot, and two roles are unnamed. The estimate is 39.0 on your phone within
days, the field test from about early December, and all 8 programmes by about the end of 2027.

## 1. What the review found in the plans docs

### Blockers (11)
- **Discreet mode:** the fix list names `plan.js` 480 and 1448, but core drops every urgent card at `app.js:1831-1832`, and
  `plan.js:1750` also returns early. (Code facts; it's the journey audit's P0 3.)
- **Reminders after a loss are a live harm today.** The pregnancy plan's reminders (check-ups, the 9th-of-the-month free
  PMSMA check-up) are only cleared from the pregnancy screens (`preg.js:959-961`), so after "The pregnancy ended" they
  keep ringing. The plans doc schedules the fix after the port. (Confirmed in the code.)
- **A stillbirth or late loss gets no recovery care:** the after-birth programme is "for a baby born alive", but her body is
  recovering from a birth (haemorrhage, pre-eclampsia, infection).
- **The methotrexate and molar gate fails open:** it depends on Sakhi knowing the kind of loss, which nothing stores.
- **Storing the kind of loss** breaks `loss.js`'s privacy rule and would travel in backups.
- **`afterProcedure`:** "Now if soaking 2 thick pads an hour" is twice the reviewed threshold, and puts bleeding after a
  birth below its reviewed urgency.
- **Chest pain** has three urgencies in the bank (Now, "Hb test this week", "easy walking").
- **Fever is missing from the pregnancy set,** and post-partum pre-eclampsia and fever from the after-birth set.
- **The heavy programme's period-day notes** ask exactly the questions that trigger core's reviewed warnings, without
  showing them.

### The main majors, grouped
- **Order and builds:**
  - the plans doc, the outline and the journey audit give three different field-test builds;
  - the safety layer comes after the port;
  - the doc says the outline's §6 to §8 stand, while the outline itself says only §0, §1 and §3 do.
- **Feasibility:**
  - `@mod:key` can't reach most reviewed lines, which are inline literals; keying them is uncosted work;
  - `check.mjs` never fails anything, so the guard the design relies on doesn't exist;
  - the golden test's 25 cases skip three live goals and several branches.
- **Rules:**
  - a PHQ-9-style active safety question (Mohit's 19 Oct scope rules out PHQ/GAD/EPDS screens);
  - a 0-to-10 flush "problem rating" mirroring HFRS;
  - leak questions mirroring ICIQ;
  - testicle lines and TB, thyroid and arthritis lines (not reproductive health);
  - helpline digits inside a programme file;
  - painkiller names reused from periods-pain;
  - Qura offer points in pregnancy and after a birth.
  - Qura's booking path sends Meta events (per `docs/qura-sakhi-integration-plan-2026-10-23.md`), which conflicts with
    "never send health data to Meta" before any new offer point is added.
- **Safety details:**
  - "Hot" water versus the reviewed "warm, not hot";
  - checking Copper-T threads (an internal check);
  - black stools from iron versus from painkillers when both run together;
  - pelvic floor levels carried from 39 weeks into the first days after a birth;
  - the 2-a-day reminder budget dropping a pregnant woman's tablet reminders;
  - the crisis sheet missing "harming my baby or someone else".
- **Minimalism:**
  - Midlife's 17 signs and 7 orders;
  - 23 pregnancy lessons that mostly repeat the 39 reviewed week tips;
  - new routines that are mostly existing reviewed steps.

The full list, with evidence and the skeptics' corrections, is in `docs/plans-review-2026-10-24-findings.md`.

## 5. Top risks

1. **Review is the critical path,** and one person may carry two-thirds of it. Name the roles now, give each a weekly slot,
   send tick-list packets, and ship label-only steps without waiting.
2. **Safety slips are live on your phone until 39.0 ships:**
   - discreet mode hides "call 112";
   - a pregnancy BP of 164/112 never reaches Today;
   - bleeding after menopause is reassured;
   - pregnancy reminders ring after a loss;
   - bleeding in pregnancy says "see a doctor today";
   - the iron set-up can't be read.
3. **Regulation:** the pilot exemption is verbal; pillar-1 signals and condition-tier programmes sit near CDSCO's triage line;
   intended use is read from the whole product, marketing included.
4. **Shared-phone privacy:** stored kind of loss (avoided), health words on the lock screen (fixed in 39.0), family cards,
   and Qura's Meta booking events.
5. **Two sources of urgency truth:** about 15 bank rows and several reviewed lines disagree. One scale, one crosswalk, the
   stricter line until the doctor decides.
6. **Port regression** in a 335 KB `plan.js` that runs your own plan: the widened golden test, one plan per commit, the cycle
   ladder untouched, the field test frozen by build.
7. **Scope creep against minimalism:** take the review's cuts, and build kit words only when a programme needs them.
8. **A field test that can't be read:** a frozen APK, process endpoints, written consent, no programme content during it.
9. **No recall and a thin test bench:** the update check before installs; an Android 10 emulator profile (newer phones only, Mohit 25 Oct); an APK size ceiling.
10. **Stored data and the DPDP rules:** a 12-month storage test, backups covering every new field, a privacy review before
    public release.
