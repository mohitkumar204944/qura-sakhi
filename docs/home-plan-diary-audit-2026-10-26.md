# Home, My plan and Diary: a clarity and daily-use audit (26 Oct 2026)

Mohit asked: is the UX the best it can be for a woman who opens Sakhi every day? Is every function clear, or can she get
confused? He wants maximum clarity on three things: what she needs to do, why she should do it, and what doing it
consistently will bring. Explore is out of scope; Home (Today), My plan and Diary are in.

Audit only. Nothing has been built.

## How I checked

I drew the app (0.39.13) in the states a woman meets most, at 390 × 844, plus Hindi at 360 × 800:
- her PMOS plan's first day;
- day 10, in the morning (nothing done), in the evening (2 of 4 done) and with all 4 done;
- week 5 (the month card and the 4-week check);
- a woman with no plan (just tracking);
- Diary: the calendar, today's day page, Cycles and Days.

I read each screen as a first-time user would: what am I meant to do here, why, and what do I get if I keep doing it?

The yardsticks are well-tested behaviour-change ideas (principle level only):
- one clear next action;
- a visible reason;
- progress she can see;
- a reward at the end of the day;
- feedback that links what she did to what changed.

Sakhi's own rules stay:
- no streaks or guilt;
- honest results, with no promises;
- few words and pictures first;
- Hindi on every screen;
- a shared phone shows nothing private.

## What already works (keep it)

- **Today has a real daily job:** 4 steps, each with its time cue ("After dinner", "At lunch or dinner"), and Start for the
  yoga.
- **The evening check-in is short.** It shows what already counted, asks only the rest, names her goal, and ends with a
  petal.
- **The rhythm beyond the day exists:**
  - the weekly look-back and Plan your week;
  - the 4-week check;
  - the 12-week path;
  - the end page.
- **Honest results:** "Periods often take 3 to 6 months to change", and results come from her own entries.
- **The Diary is rich and private:**
  - the calendar, the day page and the Cycles tab;
  - "Fertile days show after 6 cycles";
  - "What repeats for you".
- **Hindi at 360 px fits**, and a shared phone shows plain words.

## What can confuse her or lose her

### Home (Today)

1. **Five buttons look the same.** Start, Done, Done, Done and "How did today go?" are all solid pink. When everything is
   the main button, nothing is. She can't see at a glance which one is "next".
2. **A done step disappears.** Her progress vanishes with it, so she can't see what she already did today. There's no Undo
   if she tapped Done by mistake.
3. **A full day has no moment.** With all 4 done, the card shrinks to one small line, "All 4 done today", and still offers
   "How did today go?" in pink. She doesn't know if anything is left. This is the best moment of her day, and it passes
   without a word.
4. **There's no "why" on Today.** Each step's reason exists (the doctor-reviewed "why it helps" lines) but appears only
   after the check-in, one a day. "Strength day", "Cycle care yoga" and "In bed by 11:30 pm" are never explained where she
   meets them.
5. **"Day 1" sits next to "Cycle day 11".** In a period app, "Day 1" means the first day of her period. The plan's
   "Day 1 · 0 of 4 done today" (her plan's first week) will be misread.
6. **"This week" means two different weeks.**
   - Her plan's week starts on the weekday she started (a Sunday or a Saturday). "0 of 2 this week" and "5 of 7 days"
     count that week.
   - The strip at the top of Home shows the 7 days around today, and the Diary calendar runs Monday to Sunday.
   - Nothing on Home shows which 7 days "this week" is.
7. **The weekly moment is below the fold.** On the first days of a new week, the look-back ("Week 1 is done · How was your
   energy?") sits under the 4 steps and the tab bar. That's where she rates her week and plans the next one. Other cards fold
   under "More from Sakhi (2)", which few open.
8. **Five lines of text come before the first step:** greeting, plan name, week and count, check-in time, strength day. The
   strength line is bold, but she can't act on it today.
9. **A woman with no plan has no daily job.**
   - Her Home shows her cycle ring, the 3 logging buttons and "A plan for you".
   - Nothing says "log how you feel today, it takes 10 seconds" or why that matters.
   - "Why log every day?" shows once, on her first day only.

### My plan

10. **Her 4 steps aren't on My plan.** The page shows a minutes ring and one row, "Today's steps · 0 of 4", which opens Home.
    The tab named for her plan doesn't say what the plan asks of her each day, or why.
11. **The hero counts minutes, but the plan is 4 steps.** "36 of 60 minutes" and "Strength days: 0 of 2" say nothing about
    food or sleep, which are half her plan. There's no view of how consistent she has been (for example, full days this
    week).
12. **Her goal reads as a diagnosis.** The line under the title is "Week 2 of 12 · I have PMOS (PCOS)". The goal she is
    working toward ("more regular periods") isn't stated.
13. **What consistency brings is two taps away.** The honest timeline is behind the 4th tile: weeks 1 to 4 for energy and
    sleep, weeks 4 to 12 for habits and waist, 3 to 6 months for periods. The 12-week path lists minutes and new food
    habits, not what each stage tends to bring. Her own evidence is also off the page:
    - her weekly energy ratings;
    - her 4-week check;
    - her cycle gaps before and since.
14. **The page is long and has no clear centre.** About 3.5 screens: hero, today row, check-in button, "Fasting today?",
    look-back, 4-week check row, 12-week path, 4 tiles, care team, settings, garden.
15. **A morning check-in button.** At 9:30 am, with nothing done, My plan shows a big pink "How did today go?". Home rightly
    waits until the evening.
16. **Planning sits among settings.** "Plan this week" is filed under Plan settings, next to Pause and Stop. "Fasting today?"
    shows every day for everyone.
17. **The garden is at the very bottom.** The reward for checking in (a petal) points to a garden she rarely finds.

### Diary

18. **Logging a day means a long form.** The day sheet lists every section (period, sex, pain, body, mood, discharge, note).
    A daily 10-second "how do I feel" has no short path, so most days go unlogged.
19. **The value of logging every day is said once.** Two later lines are good ("4 more periods to log" for fertile days; "After 3
    periods with notes, Sakhi shows what repeats"), but they're scattered. She never sees how much she has logged this
    month, or what the next entries will unlock.
20. **The calendar legend has 11 items.** Five period marks plus six coloured dots, small and close together. That's hard to
    read in either language.
21. **Two diaries for one day.** For a woman on a plan, the plan's evening check-in and the Diary's day log are separate
    rituals. She may feel she did "the check-in" and skip the Diary, or the other way round.

### Across all three

22. **Many named features, none introduced:**
    - check-in, look-back, 4-week check;
    - strength day, rest day;
    - Plan your week, nudge;
    - Sakhi says, garden, "Change stage".

    Each is reasonable alone. Together they need one simple rhythm she learns once: every day (4 steps, evening check-in),
    every week (look-back, plan the week), every 4 weeks (check), 12 weeks (results).
23. **Impact is never tied to her own effort.** Sakhi shows what she did and, separately, how she feels. It never puts the
    two side by side. For example: "the weeks you did all 4 steps on 4 days or more, you rated your energy Good".

## Recommendations

### P0. The daily job, unmistakable (Home): Version 39.14

R1. **Today's plan becomes a checklist that stays.**
- 4 rows with a round tick on the right; the whole row is the tap target.
- A done step stays, ticked and soft, and a tap on the tick undoes it.
- The yoga row keeps a small outline Start, plus its tick.
- A 4-segment bar at the top of the card fills as she goes ("2 of 4").
- One pink button at a time: the next step's Start, or the check-in in the evening.

R2. **Close the day on Today.**
- With all 4 done, the card shows "Day complete" with:
  - her goal line ("A step towards more regular periods");
  - tomorrow in one line ("Tomorrow: Sun salutation · walk 15 minutes");
  - the petal.
- No check-in is needed when nothing is left. "Add how it went" stays as a quiet link, for the barrier question and her
  energy.

R3. **A "why" one tap away on every step.** A small "Why?" on each row opens a half sheet with three things:
- the step's reviewed reason;
- how to do it (the habit's own line, or the routine's moves);
- its tool (My plate, Yoga and moves).

The first time a strength day appears, its line has a "What's this?" with the same sheet.

R4. **"Plan day", never just "Day".** "Week 1 · plan day 1", or "Your plan: day 1". "Cycle day" stays the only plain
"day N". The same goes for Hindi.

R5. **Show her plan's week on Today.**
- 7 small dots under the plan line: her plan week, with today ringed and full days filled.
- Then "this week" always points at something she can see.
- Option for Mohit: move plan weeks to start on Monday. It's cleaner, but a bigger change: week 1 may be short, and golden is
  re-recorded.

R6. **The weekly moment above the steps.** On the first days of a new week, the look-back ("Week 1: 5 of 7 days · how was
your energy?", then Plan your week) comes inside the Today card, before the 4 steps. One card, not "More from Sakhi".

### P1. Why and what consistency brings (My plan): Version 39.15

R7. **My plan explains the plan, top to bottom.**
1. **Her goal and her week.** "Goal: more regular periods · Week 2 of 12" (no diagnosis line).
2. **Her 4 daily steps.** Each step with its one-line reason, always visible, and today's tick.
3. **This week.** 7 days, each a small 4-part ring (yoga, walk, food, sleep), so full days and missed steps show at a
   glance. Strength days are marked, with "Plan this week" right here. Minutes become a line here ("Active minutes: 36 of
   60").
4. **What consistency brings.** The reviewed timeline (weeks 1 to 4, 4 to 12, 3 to 6 months), with "You are here".
5. **Your 12 weeks**, then the tools, the care team and the settings. "Fasting today?" moves into the food step's sheet.

R8. **Her own evidence, next to the timeline, as it arrives:**
- her energy across the weeks (from the look-backs);
- her 4-week check;
- her days between periods, before and since;
- then one plain line linking effort and outcome, when there's enough data ("Weeks with 4 or more full days: energy Good 3
  of 3"). That line is new wording, so it goes to the doctor first.

R9. **The check-in button only when it's due.** Same rule as Home: from 5 pm, or once she has done a step. Before that, My
plan says "Check-in at 8 pm".

R10. **The garden next to the day's reward.** The "petal" line in the check-in opens the garden. The garden row moves up,
next to "This week".

### P2. Daily logging that takes 10 seconds (Diary and Home): Version 39.16

R11. **"How do you feel today?" in one row.**
- 5 picture chips (fine, tired, cramps, low mood, bloating), plus "More" for the full sheet.
- On Home for every woman: for a woman with no plan, this is her daily job.
- At the top of today's day sheet.
- Inside the evening check-in for a woman on a plan, so one evening ritual fills her plan and her Diary.
- Saved as today's Diary entry (`state.daily`), on the phone.

R12. **A logging meter that says what it unlocks.** At the top of Cycles: "Noted on 9 of 27 days this month", then the next
thing her logging unlocks ("2 more periods: fertile days", "1 more period with notes: what repeats for you"). It's a reason
to come back with no streak.

R13. **A legend in two lines.** The period marks, then "dots: what you noted", with a "?" that opens the full key.

### P3. Words and look (all three)

R14. **One pink button per screen.** Everything else is outline or a text link. Help and Me keep their own colour.

R15. **Name the rhythm once.** A small "How your plan works" card on My plan (and on her plan's first day):
- Every day: 4 steps, check-in at 8 pm.
- Every week: a look-back and Plan your week.
- Every 4 weeks: a 1-minute check.
- After 12 weeks: your results.

Use the same words everywhere after that.

## Build order (each one tested at 360 × 780, in Hindi and on a shared phone)

| Version | What | Size |
|---|---|---|
| 39.14 | R1 to R6: the checklist, a day that closes itself, a "why" per step, "plan day", the week dots, the weekly moment on Today | Medium (Today's card, the check-in rule, tests that tap Done) |
| 39.15 | R7 to R10: My plan explains the plan; this week as rings; what consistency brings; her evidence | Medium (My plan's page, golden's My plan text) |
| 39.16 | R11 to R13: the 10-second daily log, the logging meter, the short legend | Small to medium (Home, the day sheet, Cycles) |
| any | R14, R15: one primary button, the rhythm card | Small |

**Health wording:** R3 and R7 reuse the reviewed reasons and the reviewed timeline. R8's effort-and-outcome line is new and
goes to the doctor. R11's chips use the Diary's existing symptom words.

## Decisions for Mohit

1. **Done steps stay visible, ticked (recommended), or keep disappearing?**
2. **A full day closes itself (recommended), or does she still check in every evening?** With R2, the check-in is only for
   steps she didn't tick.
3. **Plan weeks: keep her start day and show the dots (recommended for now), or move to Monday weeks?**
4. **The 10-second "How do you feel today?" row:** on Home for every woman (recommended), or only in the Diary?
5. **Order:** Today first (39.14, recommended), then My plan (39.15), then the Diary (39.16)? Or My plan first?
